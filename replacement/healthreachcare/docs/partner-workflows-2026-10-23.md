# Connected partner workflow update

This update modifies the existing Sites app. Firebase Authentication remains the identity provider; application records remain in the existing D1 workspace and private files in R2. No Firebase users, customer records, DNS, billing configuration or old Cloudflare deployment were deleted or migrated.

## Implemented

- Patient home headings removed as requested. The fourth card opens an existing prescription catalogue route with prescription filtering and ordering/review guidance. Empty inventory remains empty; no fake medication is inserted.
- Production Homebase policy (`HRC_HOMEBASE_ALLOWLIST=true`) permits only Firebase-verified `hlawulekanimalunagani12@gmail.com` and `support@healthreachcare.co.za`. It overrides legacy UID registries and administrator claims. Delegation/ownership-transfer endpoints are disabled under this policy. No role is derived from editable profile fields. Existing Firebase sign-in is required.
- New partner profiles enter Pending verification automatically. Pending/rejected/incomplete partners can open their own dashboards and prepare a service profile without submitting documents or patient onboarding. Backend operational actions remain blocked until account approval.
- Authorized administrators can approve/reject, suspend, hold, reactivate and soft-delete partners through Users & providers → Review profile. Reasons are required except for approval. Approval requires confirmation that necessary checks were performed. Soft deletion preserves the Firebase user, documents, transactions and audit history, and blocks application access. No permanent Auth deletion is attempted.
- Doctor and homecare categories share the patient discovery definitions. Providers can save multiple categories/services, actual prices, service areas and working hours. Approved, complete and accepting profiles publish without a second mandatory publication review. A deliberate administrator listing hold remains effective. Doctor credentials and saved availability are still required; approval is not a regulatory verification service.
- Pharmacy products use persisted owner-scoped CRUD, stock/price validation and publication switches. Multiple product images reuse the existing R2 validation and ownership rules. Only attached images on active, published, approved catalogue products are publicly served; private documents never use this route. Checkout snapshots and prescription review remain intact; no pharmacy quotation process is added.
- Existing delivery tasks now support authenticated availability, eligible offer acceptance/decline, exclusive assignment via workspace compare-and-swap, progress and separate pickup/delivery handover codes. Saved driver cities control area eligibility. Genuine optional driver coordinates support the existing proximity display; no coordinate/distance is invented. Location/ETA updates use existing delivery tracking and expire/stop when the delivery ends. Assigned drivers retain only redacted delivery records.
- Driver/patient and driver/pharmacy text conversations are transaction-scoped. Completion archives conversations. No fake message, read status, booking or payment is generated.
- Account status changes update provider/driver eligibility on the next shared workspace read. The current interface refreshes persisted data; this update does not claim Firestore realtime subscriptions.
- Test payments remain explicitly simulated. Confirmations do not represent collected money or withdrawable funds. Provider decline returns reserved stock and preserves the payment/audit record without inventing a refund.

## Manual connected test steps

1. Sign in at `/login` using each allowed email, verify it with Firebase, and confirm routing to `/dashboard/admin/overview`. If a listed account is not registered, its owner must register it and complete Firebase email verification. An email address alone does not establish that an account exists. Check that other verified users cannot open Homebase or its protected APIs.
2. Create each partner through `/partners`, verify email, and enter its own dashboard. Confirm operational writes fail while pending. Sign in with either Homebase account, open Users & providers → Review profile, confirm appropriate checks and approve.
3. As Doctor, open Account → Professional profile → Practice settings (or Edit public profile), acknowledge the applicable policies, select patient categories, enter credentials/services/fee/hours and save. Configure the saved appointment calendar. As Patient choose a matching category and actual slot, review the price and complete demo payment. Verify doctor acceptance/decline, scheduling, contextual messages and completion from the correct accounts.
4. As Pharmacy save profile/hours/service area and delivery fee, add an in-stock product, attach images and publish. As Patient choose an address in that area, add products, review totals and complete demo checkout. Verify the pharmacy queue, processing and snapshot preservation after a price change. Prescription items must undergo document and pharmacy verification before payment.
5. As Homecare save categories/services with positive hourly rates, area and availability. As Patient select that service, choose a future available date/time and address, review total and use demo payment. Verify provider acceptance/decline, scheduling, assignment and completion. If no actual provider exists, the app saves an unpaid availability request; it does not manufacture a confirmed booking.
6. As approved Driver set actual cities and go online. Advance a paid delivery order to Ready for dispatch as its pharmacy. Accept the offer; verify a second driver cannot claim it. Enter the pharmacy's pickup code and the patient's delivery code at the correct stages. Share location only with permission, save ETA, verify patient progress and confirm tracking disappears at completion.
7. Put a provider on hold or suspend it through Homebase. Refresh patient discovery and the provider dashboard. Verify it disappears from new-work eligibility and operational writes are denied. Reactivate after review; existing business records remain intact.

## Validation and limits

Automated tests exercise API actions, route/access guards, saved data projection, UI handlers, prescription preservation, image ownership, payments, provider queues, delivery handovers, location expiry, administrator allowlisting and account control using isolated fictional fixtures and mocked Firebase identities. Run `npm test`, `npm run lint`, `npx tsc --noEmit` and the production build.

Actual sign-in to the two specified inboxes, email receipt, genuine providers/inventory, browser geolocation permission on users' devices and physical delivery require owner/provider testing. No passwords or email codes should be shared in chat. Email confirmation on every administrator login is not implemented; existing Firebase sign-in/email verification and recent-auth checks remain. Live payment gateways, payouts/bank verification, external professional checks, authenticated message audio/attachment integration and the separate Cloudflare-domain cutover remain outside the verified functionality of this update. No claim of production readiness or regulatory certification is made.
