# Patient medication, Homecare and delivery changes

Scope is the requested patient experience. Patient navigation remains Home, Activities, Notifications, Account. The selected Firebase project remains gen-lang-client-0810450473. No old Firebase connection, DNS change, database reset or account migration was performed.

Removed the entire Patient Home map widget and its location controls. Reminders are followed by Recent Orders/activity. The first three service cards and their copy/destinations remain unchanged. The fourth prescription shortcut now opens the normal medication catalogue, labelled Prescription medication. Legacy prescription URLs show the same catalogue, not a standalone prescription service.

Prescription-only products can be added to the regular basket. Continuing a basket containing prescription items saves a prescription draft with its products, quantities, fulfilment, address and total, then opens the existing documents-only step within that medication journey. The platform's current checks require prescription, ID front/back and selfie; these are platform verification rules, not a claim that every South African prescription legally requires a selfie. Explicit submission sends the unpaid record and a notification to its pharmacy. Only that approved pharmacy can review identity and approve/reject the prescription. Approval uses its saved catalogue prices and leaves the record unpaid; a rejected record cannot be approved through the same completed review. No payment or dispatch is invented.

Restored Homecare's existing fixed-price HomecareBooking form in place of the new-request quotation form. Historical quotation records remain readable and unchanged. The real-account view does not expose test-payment outcome controls or simulate payment success.

Medication order details now show status-based progress, a recent assigned-driver street map using OpenStreetMap and an arrival estimate when the assigned driver has saved one. Driver updates use actual device geolocation after permission; verified driver identity, assignment, payment, prescription safeguards and active-delivery status are checked. Points expire after two minutes and disappear when delivery ends. The progress icon reflects saved status, not elapsed-time fabrication. Estimates are explicitly driver-supplied, not inferred road travel times. No instant-delivery guarantee is made.

Validation: full regression suite passed, including the original 1255 isolated API workflow checks and unchanged four-item patient navigation. Added 23 tracking/arrival/permission/fulfilment guard checks; 9 order-map/Homecare/prescription rendering checks; and expanded Firebase workspace tests to 110 assertions including upload persistence, unchanged selections/address/totals, pharmacy receipt/notification, unauthorized review refusal, identity approval, fixed catalogue total and rejection guards. Authentication and storage in these tests are mocked/in-memory; no real medical documents, drivers or payments were created. Lint and TypeScript passed; production build outcome is reported after execution.

Remaining limits: real payment processing, newly assigned live delivery operations, real stocked/published providers and an actual driver GPS run have not been verified. The map is a genuine street map with real-coordinate support, not proof a live delivery exists. Real-account unsupported payment/booking operations remain unavailable rather than returning demo success. No unrelated account/login/doctor redesign was made.
