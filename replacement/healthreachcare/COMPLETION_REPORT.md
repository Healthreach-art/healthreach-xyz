# HealthReachCare demo release — 22 October 2026 (South Africa)

This release updates the existing private HealthReachCare Site. It preserves the project, existing workspace records, D1/R2 bindings and ChatGPT sign-in. **Production Firebase configuration, DNS, production repository and live customer data were not changed.** Every account, payment, professional check, delivery and settlement described here is a demo operation.

## Marketplace and account completion in this release

- Added dedicated protected Onboarding, Public Profile, Payments and Payouts, Security, Privacy and Registration routes. General Account links to them. Role-conditional onboarding fields show progress, missing information and evidence/declaration state; new provider personas enter onboarding directly.
- New provider approval and public-profile publication are distinct. Approved applicants prepare a public profile and submit it for Admin publication or requested changes. Unpublished applicants cannot receive new checkout, prescription, appointment or service requests. Existing built-in fictional preview providers are explicitly retained as Sample preview; this migration does not invent registry checks or approval history for them.
- Provider public profiles store descriptions, languages, service areas, operating times, optional coordinates and radius. Doctor discovery adds consultation-mode, maximum-fee and language filters. No invented provider photographs are supplied.
- Configured pharmacy matching uses current approval/publication, stock, hours, saved service area/radius, exact product identity, displayed price and fulfilment mode. Prices and delivery fee remain fixed across fallback. Order acceptance has a five-minute deadline. Decline/timeout can reroute an Order, moving its stock reservation and provider-net ledger allocation, archiving prior conversations and updating scoped notifications. No eligible replacement produces Awaiting pharmacy, preserving payment and history without dispatch. Patient retry is explicit. Clinical product identity is frozen for matching; no medicine substitution is inferred.
- Prescription intake uses matching before review. An approved prescription is never silently transferred to another pharmacy; the required identity/prescription checks still precede payment and fulfilment.
- Homecare matching uses saved area/coordinates, offered service, hours and interval overlap; it preserves the separate quotation workflow. A provider is treated as one scheduling capacity unit in this demo, rather than pretending named staff capacity has been verified.
- Added masked test payout beneficiary profiles with five-minute, single-use simulated security challenges, lockout after five wrong attempts, ownership checks, review history and explicit simulated verification. Raw real account numbers are rejected. Editing details resets verification; challenge hashes and account-reference fingerprints are never returned. This is not real bank verification or external reauthentication.
- Added weekly-period settlement Draft → Reviewed → Approved → Initiated → Simulated completed / Completed with failures, reconciliation before initiation, account-detail version snapshots, duplicate protection and failed-item retry through a fresh referenced batch. Periods use Africa/Johannesburg boundaries, with Friday as the default weekly preference. CSV statements are available per batch; spreadsheet formula prefixes are escaped. Initiation/outcomes remain explicit Admin simulations, not an automatic bank transfer or closed-app scheduled job.
- Added immutable payment fee snapshots, fee ledger legs, and platform-funded fixed pharmacy promotion codes with expiry/use limits. A failed payment consumes no promotion use. Confirmed prices/fees are preserved when later settings change.
- Added requested partial/full refunds with Pending, Failed, Rejected and Simulated refunded outcomes. Pending review does not invent a refund. Saved references, remaining-amount checks, fee/promotion reversal and ledger reconciliation prevent duplicate or excessive refunds. Partial refunds retain fulfilment; a full pre-fulfilment refund releases stock and cancels dispatch tasks.
- Corrected delivery-pharmacy conversation participants, pickup names and event-recipient scoping for multiple pharmacies. Kept the compact test-workspace notice, test payment/verification boundaries and sample inventory labels; simplified routine customer copy.

## Demo integration boundaries and remaining limitations

- Sign-in is the existing Sites ChatGPT identity. Additional registration and six-role switching create personas inside one owner's private testing workspace, not real independently authenticated Firebase users. The Admin test persona is provisioned by the demo; profile editing cannot create Admin.
- Email challenges display an explicitly simulated code and never claim an email was sent. Provider registry checks are named **Simulated verified**. All payments, payouts and adjustments are test accounting entries; nothing connects to a bank or moves money.
- Fixed offers are the defined dispatch mode. Competitive bidding is deliberately not enabled; there is no decorative Submit Bid action. Live road routing, GPS, external map-app results and courier operation are unverified/not configured. Saved-coordinate distances are mathematical straight-line estimates, not driving distances.
- The optional licensed image-search source has no configured provider credentials, so the UI explains its unavailability. Upload/gallery functionality is implemented. No arbitrary website scraping or unlicensed image copying is used.
- Private-file retention is a demo policy (identity/professional test evidence seven days, prescription/appointment/support evidence thirty days, measured from upload). Expired access is denied and Admin purge excludes legal holds. Automatic physical deletion while the app is closed is not scheduled. A production retention/legal-hold design needs professional review.
- The financial ledger is append-only via the authorized application API, not an immutable external financial database. Commission basis points, variable/fixed processing fees and clearance are configurable; fees are frozen per successful payment. Real fee agreements, payment callbacks/chargebacks/disputes, reconciled processor statements, banking details and actual payouts need an authorized provider and production implementation.
- Multiple provider accounts and provider/record ownership are represented. Staff-specific invitation, delegated staff permissions and staffing-capacity scheduling are not production-ready. Do not treat a fictional caregiver note as a verified staff identity.
- Appointment reminders are in-app events generated while the shared workspace is accessed/polled; no background email/SMS/push scheduler or live video consultation is configured.
- Real geocoding, external email/SMS/push, external identity/credential validation, production staff provisioning, real prescriptions/clinical processing and real payment services remain disabled/unconfigured. The demo must not receive real medical, identity or banking information.
- The single-row aggregate workspace, hosted authentication trust boundary, incident-response process, database/storage permissions, long-term health-record retention and data-subject deletion need a separate production architecture/security/privacy review. Draft legal documents do not establish legal, POPIA, clinical, pharmacy or payment compliance.
- The separate `Healthreach-art/healthreach-xyz` production repository/Firebase project remains unconnected and unchanged. Its code/rules/indexes/deployment, real user accounts and production data have not been inspected or tested.

## Payment integration research boundary

Official [Paystack split-payment documentation](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/split-payments/) and [multi-split documentation](https://paystack.com/docs/payments/multi-split-payments/) describe subaccount settlement splits. Official [Payfast split payments](https://payfast.io/features/split-payments/) and [merchant FAQs](https://payfast.io/faq/merchant-faqs/) describe its split-payment feature and account-side enablement. These are candidates for separately authorized provider due diligence; neither is configured in this demo. Split settlement is not assumed to authorize escrow or HealthReachCare holding customer money. Country/account eligibility, healthcare merchant acceptance, contractual payout timing, refunds, bank validation and sandbox callbacks require direct provider verification before implementation with real credentials.
